Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

LinguEd Center collects the information you provide in the assessment booking form—such as your name, WhatsApp number, optional email address, test, target score, deadline, preferred format, and appointment time—to arrange and follow up on your Test Readiness Assessment.

How we use your information

We use it to confirm your appointment, prepare an appropriate assessment, contact you about your request, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and access

Booking information may be stored using service providers used by LinguEd, including Google services. Access is limited to people who need the information to arrange or deliver your assessment.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your booking information by contacting us on WhatsApp at +250 793 520 361.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact LinguEd Center using the WhatsApp number above.
